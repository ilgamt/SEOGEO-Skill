# Search data and GEO checks

## Google and Yandex owner data

- In Search Console and Yandex Webmaster, compare important URL inventory with indexed, excluded, crawled, and discovered groups. For key URLs inspect the actual page status, last crawl, and selected canonical where supported. Google `Crawled - currently not indexed` does not itself identify low-quality content; `Discovered - currently not indexed` means not yet crawled.
- For Yandex `LOW_DEMAND` ("Малоценная или маловостребованная"), cluster excluded URLs by page type and business importance. Check duplicates, parameters, visibility to the robot, internal links, demand, and unique user value before choosing improvement, consolidation, canonical, redirect, noindex, or crawl control. It is a page status, not proof of a sitewide penalty. Reindexing and traffic after edits are not guaranteed.
- Compare queries, pages, clicks, impressions, CTR, positions, devices, countries/regions, and periods within each source. For audit reports, default to the last 6 months and provide month-by-month tables for Google Search Console, Yandex Webmaster, Yandex Metrica, GA/analytics, AI visibility, and traffic/conversion sections whenever available. Build comparable Google and Yandex performance tables side by side using each platform's closest available metrics. Search Console omits anonymized queries and may return only top rows; do not call an export the complete keyword universe. Do not equate search clicks with analytics sessions.
- If a UI only shows a shorter period, totals without monthly rows, or a limited sample, state the limitation and name the exact export/API needed. Do not replace the 6-month monthly table with a single total. Totals may be included only in addition to monthly detail.
- For Yandex, use applicable site diagnostics, server response, crawl statistics, mobile check, JS-rendering check, title/description duplicate report, GET-parameter settings, and important-page monitoring. Confirm surprising results with HTML or logs.
- Use Wordstat and other demand or backlink sources when the question calls for them; record geography, period, data provider, sample size, and cost. Do not treat search frequency as a traffic forecast or a link sample as the whole profile.

For content performance decline, query/page cannibalization, programmatic pages, release comparisons, and scoped SEO/ads overlap, use [growth-and-quality.md](growth-and-quality.md). Preserve the 6-month monthly core report; add quarter/year comparisons only with suitable history. Missing data is a limitation, not a zero value.

## AI answer visibility

- Keep SEO foundations first: discoverability, indexability, useful original content, clear facts, and credible evidence. Google says no special AI file or schema is required for AI Overviews/AI Mode; `llms.txt` and a vendor GEO score are not established Google ranking factors.
- With owner access, inspect Yandex Webmaster's "Видимость сайта в Алисе AI": Share of Voice, trend, example queries and cited sites. It covers a limited query set related to the site's search presence, not all AI answers or referral traffic. In Google Search Console inspect the separate Generative AI performance report for AI Overviews/AI Mode impressions, pages, countries, devices, and dates if shown for the property. Its absence or insufficient data is a limitation, not zero AI visibility; its impressions are not a count of citations or visits.
- The Alice AI visibility report does not provide impression or click counts. With Yandex Metrica, inspect referrals/labels and landing pages, but do not label all Yandex organic visits as Alice AI traffic without a distinct signal.
- For any cross-platform prompt sample, record the exact query, market/language, platform/model, date, repetitions, whether the brand was mentioned, whether a clickable source URL was cited, and which URL. Answers vary; brand mention, source citation, and visit are different outcomes. Do not combine platform results into a universal percentage.
- For ChatGPT Search, check OAI-SearchBot access and, with site analytics, referrals/`utm_source=chatgpt.com`, landing pages, and goals. Filter separately tagged paid ad traffic. Referrals are not a complete count of appearances or citations in answers.
- If testing AI-bot access, distinguish search/citation bots, training bots, and user-triggered agents and respect the site's chosen policy. Check actual logs or documented crawler behavior; robots.txt allowance alone does not prove the bot fetched a page. Do not generate AI-specific endpoints, alter robots.txt, or publish generated claims merely to raise a third-party readiness score. Fixed passage lengths and GEO scores are editorial heuristics, not measured probabilities of citation.

## GEO audit tasks

Use this task map when the user asks for GEO, AI visibility, AI citations, Agentic Web readiness, or "how to make AI recommend/cite the site." GEO here means evidence-based readiness for generative answer engines, not a guarantee of AI ranking, citation, or traffic.

1. **AI-видимость за последние 6 месяцев.** Build month-by-month tables for every available platform: Google Generative AI performance in GSC, Yandex Webmaster "Видимость сайта в Алисе AI", ChatGPT/Search referrals in analytics, and any explicitly tested third-party AI search. For each platform record period, metric definitions, shown pages/queries, brand mentions, source URLs, countries/devices if available, and limitations. If a platform lacks monthly data, say exactly which export/API/manual sample is needed.
2. **Пул GEO-запросов.** Create a query set that reflects how people ask AI systems, not only classic SEO keywords. Split by intent: commercial choice (`где заказать`, `кого выбрать`, `лучшая компания`), informational advice (`как выбрать`, `что лучше`), local/service-area queries, brand queries, comparison queries, problem-solution queries, and price/timeline queries. For every prompt record language, region, target page, business value, and expected answer type.
3. **Страницы-источники для AI-ответов.** For each important page type (home, service, geo, guide, FAQ, portfolio/case, contacts, about, pricing/terms if present), check whether an AI system can extract a short trustworthy answer: who the company is, what it does, where it works, for whom, price/range where appropriate, timelines, guarantees, proof, examples, contacts, and next step. Mark missing facts as page-level tasks with full URLs.
4. **Цитируемость и упоминания бренда.** For each tested AI answer, record exact prompt, platform/model/product, date, region/language, repetitions, whether the brand is mentioned, whether the site is cited with a clickable URL, which URL is cited, which competitors are cited, and what facts are used. Separate brand mention, source citation, and referral visit; they are different outcomes.
5. **Машиночитаемый слой.** Check whether structured data matches visible content and supports the page's role: `Organization`/`LocalBusiness`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Product`/`Offer` only where real, reviews/ratings only when visible and policy-compliant, contacts, areas served, sameAs profiles, and canonical URLs. Do not add schema that invents facts or hides content from users.
6. **Доступ AI-ботов и поисковых роботов.** Check robots.txt, meta robots, X-Robots-Tag, canonical, sitemap, WAF/CDN, geo-block, rate limits, JS rendering, and logs where available for Googlebot, YandexBot, OAI-SearchBot/SearchGPT-style crawlers, and other relevant citation/search bots. Distinguish real verified bot access from user-agent spoofing.
7. **Фактическая база сайта.** Audit whether the site contains clear, sourceable facts: company identity, legal/business name where relevant, address/regions, phone/email, service list, differentiators, materials/technologies, photos/cases, staff/expertise, warranties, delivery/service terms, prices or ranges if business allows, FAQ, and up-to-date dates. Each missing fact becomes a content task tied to URLs.
8. **Внешние подтверждения.** Check whether public entities corroborate the site's facts: maps, directories, review platforms, social profiles, media, partner pages, backlinks, and local NAP consistency. For each mismatch list source URL, field, site value, external value, and required correction. Treat external references as corroboration signals, not direct proof of AI ranking.
9. **Конкуренты в AI-ответах.** For the same GEO prompt pool, record which competitors appear, which URLs/sources are cited, what facts or proof make them usable, and what the audited site lacks. Convert gaps into page-level tasks: missing comparison facts, lack of cases, weak local proof, absent FAQ, unclear offer, or insufficient external corroboration.
10. **Разрывы источников AI-цитирований.** Extract full cited URLs from the saved prompt sample, distinguish owned, competitor, and independent sources, inspect the facts used, cluster source types, and assess realistic profile/content/publication opportunities. Record platform, prompt, date, source URL, missing fact, feasibility, and detailed action. Use the procedure in [growth-and-quality.md](growth-and-quality.md#разрывы-в-источниках-ai-цитирований); a source citation in the sample does not prove a ranking factor.
11. **Страницы с подтвержденными AI-переходами.** Analyze identifiable referrals/UTM by platform and landing URL, monthly for 6 months where history exists, with engagement and conversions under each analytics system's definitions. Inspect useful facts, format, links, and CTA on these pages and propose a test on comparable pages. Use [growth-and-quality.md](growth-and-quality.md#страницы-с-подтвержденными-ai-переходами); unavailable referrers or history must remain explicit limitations.
12. **GEO-план задач к действию.** End the GEO section with a detailed action plan: finding/task IDs, priority, URL/template, prompt/query group, concrete content/schema/technical/external-profile work, owner role, dependencies, baseline, readiness and success criteria, leading indicator, failure/reassessment signal, and verification source/date or event. Follow [growth-and-quality.md](growth-and-quality.md#проверяемые-критерии-задач). Include these tasks in the full audit's final `План задач к действию`; they must be executable without re-reading the audit and must not promise AI citation as a guaranteed result.

## Paid ChatGPT ads: optional, separate channel

- If the user requests a paid-channel or full acquisition audit and has Ads Manager data, review own campaigns' impressions, clicks, spend, CTR, CPC/CPM, and attributed conversions. Reconcile ad URL tracking with site analytics, accounting for attribution settings, date range, time zone, and reporting delays.
- Never use Ads Manager metrics as evidence of organic ChatGPT mentions, citations, or ranking. OpenAI says ads are separate from and do not influence ChatGPT answers. Missing Ads Manager access is not an SEO defect. Check current advertiser-country eligibility before recommending use, and do not create campaigns, tracking pixels, or spend money without a separate explicit request.

## Paid placements near Alice AI: optional, separate channel

- Yandex documents ads in the "Поиск с Алисой" tab and experimental, marked promo units in Alice AI chat. If the user requests paid-channel analysis and has Yandex Direct access, inspect campaign reporting and Metrica visits/conversions separately from Webmaster's organic Alice AI SoV.
- Confirm whether the current Direct account/report can isolate Alice AI placement before attributing any impressions, clicks, cost, or conversions to it. If not, report only the broader Direct campaign result and state that Alice-specific measurement is unavailable. Do not infer organic citations from ad delivery or create campaigns without an explicit request.
